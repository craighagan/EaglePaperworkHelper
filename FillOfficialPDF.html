Fill the Official Eagle Workbook PDF

Copy your workbook answers into the official Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook.

This page never sends your answers anywhere. The finished PDF holds personal details, so it goes wherever you copy or share it. The only thing this page can download is the blank official workbook from scouting.org, and only if you click “Get the official PDF for me.”

Step 1. Get the official workbook PDF

This fills the official Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook No. 2023a from Scouting America.

Not working, or no internet? Download the official PDF when you can. It may open in a browser tab instead of downloading: on a computer click the download icon (⤓); on an iPhone or iPad tap the Share button, then Save to Files. Then come back and choose that file with “I already have it.”

Step 2. Choose your answers

Choose the backup file you saved with Save backup file in a workbook page. It ends in .json and is usually in your Downloads folder. Keep that file somewhere safe, such as Google Drive or Dropbox.

Step 3. Check the project name

This goes on the cover page. To keep a change, also type it in the workbook page (Proposal → Project name).

Step 4. Create the filled PDF

Your browser saves a new copy of the official workbook with your answers typed in. The PDF you chose is not changed.

Step 5 (optional). Make a submission copy with your signed pages

This makes one more PDF: the whole filled workbook, with each signed page you added put right after the official page it belongs to, then a page that lists your attachments and links, then the attachments. Nothing is replaced. Add scans and photos on the workbook’s Signed Pages tab, then save a backup file.

Choose your answers in Step 2 first.

Use it only when someone asks for the whole workbook with its signatures in one file. Ask your unit leader or council what they want. The submission copy is a printed copy: its boxes can’t be edited and it can’t be turned back into a backup file. Keep your backup file as your working copy, and make a new submission copy after you change an answer or add a signed page.

Both the submission copy and your backup file hold pictures of the adults’ signatures as well as your personal details. Share them only with your parent or guardian, your unit leader, and the people reviewing your project. Ask your unit leader or council how they want things turned in; if they want only a signed page, send the scan or photo you took.
